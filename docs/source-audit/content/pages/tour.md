##### A Refreshing Welcome: Organic Fruit Juice  _(H3)_
Your adventure begins with a warm Balinese welcome and a refreshing glass of organic fruit juice. Sourced from local farms, this delightful beverage is a taste of the tropical paradise that surrounds you. Sip slowly and let the vibrant flavors awaken your senses.

##### Visit our Temples  _(H3)_
Each Balinese village has 3 temples, and every family has their own Family Temple. Wayan your tour guide will introduce to you a day in the life of a Balinese family including in-depth explanations of the religious significance of each temple and the importance of the Ancestors in Balinese culture. Throughout the tour he will educate and amuse you with anecdotes of how Balinese Spirituality is used on a daily basis in the village.

##### Explore the Farm: A Hands-On Tour  _(H3)_
Next, take a guided tour of our sprawling organic farm. Here, you’ll discover the harmony of a thriving ecosystem that sustains itself without the need for harmful chemicals. Learn about traditional and modern farming techniques as you stroll through lush vegetable patches, fruit orchards, and herb gardens.

##### Feed the Animals !  _(H3)_
Kids and adults of all ages love to get close up to our amazing farm animals. You will get to meet Susu & Taksu our cows, Happy & Lili the farm cats, Adam & Eve the ducks, our turtles, fish and the occasional monkey !

##### Rice Paddy Walk: A Journey Through Tradition  _(H3)_
Bali’s iconic rice paddies are a sight to behold, and a guided walk through these emerald terraces offers insight into the island’s agricultural heritage. Discover the Subak irrigation system, a UNESCO-recognized tradition that reflects the Balinese philosophy of harmony between humans, nature, and spirit.

##### Jungle Tour: Reconnect with Nature  _(H3)_
Venture into the heart of Bali’s lush jungle, where towering trees, vibrant flora, and melodious bird songs create a serene escape. Learn about the native plants and their traditional uses in Balinese medicine and daily life. This tour is a meditative experience, inviting you to reconnect with the natural world.
