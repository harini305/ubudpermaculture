#### Our Story  _(H2)_
My name is Wayan and I run the Community and Villas with my brother Nyoman and our extended families.

We started Ubud Permaculture Community on our family land during COVID as we all lost our jobs in tourism.

Next time you are in Ubud please pop in to see us and I would love to share with you the inside story of a day in the life of our very traditional Balinese village.

Why not try one of our unique Tours and Cooking Classes ?

Hi everyone – I am Nyoman and I will be guiding your tour.

You will be meeting Susu & Taksu our friendly cows, Happy & Lili our farm cats, Adam & Eve and our extended duckling family, turtles, fish and the occasional monkey !

  - image: https://ubudpermaculture.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Duck-Closeup-150x150.jpg (alt="", 150x150)
  - image: https://ubudpermaculture.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/6-150x150.jpg (alt="", 150x150)
Our farm is totally based on Permaculture Organic principles and we have over 70 varieties of fruit and vegetables to tend daily. A typical day starts as the sun rises by feeding the animals and watering the gardens, before collecting the manure from Susu’s house to make compost. I will share with you all of the permaculture knowledge that we have picked up in the last few years,

Take a look around and don’t be shy.
