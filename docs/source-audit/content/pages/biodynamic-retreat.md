Permaculture offers clues and guidance on how to make your life fit to the place where you live. It isn´t a new idea. Indigenous cultures have been living sustainably in their ancestral territories for thousands of years. Permaculture then, is a way of offering to those of us not fortunate enough to have been born into a sustainable civilization a model for how to live sustainably in one place. After witnessing too many permaculture course s with too little meaningful hands – on experience, we a re offering this re treat as a deep dive in to practical application of Balinese traditions, Biodynamic techniques and Permaculture principles. You will be guide d by expert instruction from regenerative farmers , sharing what they’ve learned over decades in the field. The curriculum is organized around 6 modules (20 hours) combining lectures, videos, hands-on and practical demonstrations, giving each participant an opportunity to learn at their own pace. This 3 days retreat offer you the opportunity to : – Observe and learn from numerous well-established permaculture systems in action – Discover and understand our farm, rainforest and rice paddies with a new vision – Connect with a network of local and international gardeners, builders and designers – Participate in installations, hands-on activities and collaborative workshops – Acquire a certificate and a toolbox of permaculture techniques, strategies, solutions, resources, and skills. The skills you will learn are valued all over the world , and will be needed to re-design and improve many aspects of your life, from your home to your livelihood, relationships, and community.

Biodynamic Retreat – Curriculum Outline The curriculum is divided into 6 modules over 20 hours. Each module reinforces the knowledge gained in earlier sessions – as spiral learning: each subject is related to the others. By the end of the course, this whole-systems approach will give you regenerative tools to improve your life.

Module 1: Biodynamic and Permaculture – History, Ethics, and principles – Pattern understanding

Module 2: The Permaculture Design – Elements and functions – Zone planning – Use of Natural resources

Module 3: Soil (where it all begins!) – pH – soil problems – regenerating and mineralizing soil

Module 4: Water management – Calculating catchment – Ponds and dams – slopes, terraces and swales

Module 5: What to plant where? – Cool to hot climates – Climatic factors – Altitude – Prevalent winds – Maximising sunshine

Module 6: Animals as a fundamental part of the system – recycling manure for compost – helping control pests

CONCEPTS Rudolf Steiner’s Biodynamic Permaculture:

History, Philosophy & Principles – Patterns in Nature – Regenerative design – Energy Storage and Resources

ECOLOGICAL THEMES IN DESIGN Soil, where it all begins – Trees and Forests – Water – Climate

CULTIVATED ECOLOGY Soil building techniques – Forest gardens – vegetable gardens – Nursery care –  raised beds – renewable energy systems – waste recycling – water management –  Integrated Pest Management – climate-appropriate strategies – Natural Buildings – Urban Permaculture

DESIGN Intro to Design – Process and Methodologies of design – Techniques and tools for Design Strategies

SOCIAL PERMACULTURE Integrating with your Community – Education in the wider Community – Embracing the Arts, Music & Dance

HANDS-ON LEARNING This course relies heavily on regular Hands on sessions introducing you to the the basic tools & techniques of applying Permaculture to food cultivation.

Soil testing – Seed Planting – Composting strategies – Organic Pesticides and Fertilizer – Farm Animals

These sessions will allow attendees to see a working farm in action and get their hands dirty !

#### FAQs  _(H2)_
- Can I attend the PDC and stay in my own accommodation ?
Answer – Yes! Participants are free to choose their own accommodation, on or off site. However, the course begins right after breakfast at 9am.

- Do I need any prerequisites or a particular background, in order to participate in the PDC ?
Answer – There are no prerequisites except for a basic knowledge of the English language as this course will be taught in English. This Permaculture retreat is tailored for people of all ages and educational backgrounds.

- When exactly is it ?
Answer – This retreat starts on Sunday morning the 9th (9am) until Tuesday the 11th (3pm) of November. Classes start at 9am until 1pm; lunch (free buffet) is served until 2pm;

Classes start again at 2pm until 5:30pm (3 pm on the last day) Weather-wise, outdoor activities take place after 2pm.

- Will I be certified at the end of the course ?
Answer – Every participant is eligible for certification provided the minimum percentage of modules are attended (80%).

- Is there any age criteria for the course ?
Answer – The minimum age set for this course in particular is 15 years so as to ascertain that the participant is mature enough for understanding and applying this knowledge. Shorter courses are available for younger permaculture enthusiasts.

- Is this a theoretical or practical course ?
Answer – A Permaculture Design Certificate course is rather theory-heavy (72 hours)

This retreat therefore is practice oriented through workshops, hands-on and demonstrations.

- Are there internships and/or volunteer opportunities to build on practical knowledge after the course ?
Answer – Yes, there are many volunteer opportunities and internships in organisations, farms and communities across Bali. Facilitators will be sharing contacts by the end of the course so interested participants can deepen their practice.

If you would like to volunteer at Ubud Permaculture please talk to Nyoman or check out the WWOOFers site !

Answer – After this 3 days biodynamic retreat, you will see the world around you with different eyes. Thousands of change-makers, educators, consultants and activists around the world are actively practising Biodynamic techniques and influencing the decisions of major corporations, small businesses and people to change the way we live. With Permaculture, we can now grow the revolution that our planet needs.
