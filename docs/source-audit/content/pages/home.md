#### Welcome to Ubud Permaculture!  _(H2)_
Welcome to our little secret hideaway hidden in the jungle just 5km north of Ubud Bali. If you have a few hours to spare while you are in Ubud and don’t wish to spend hours in the car then pop in to see us.

- Cooking Classes – from 4 – 5hrs
  - link: "Cooking Classes" → https://ubudpermaculture.simplybook.asia/v2/#book/category/6/count/1/

- Cultural Activities – from 1 – 3hrs
  - link: "Cultural Activities" → https://ubudpermaculture.simplybook.asia/v2/#book/category/2/
- Childrens Activities – from 1 – 3hrs

  - link: "Fun Activities" → https://ubudpermaculture.simplybook.asia/v2/#book/category/5/
To get here we recommend guests use Gojek (for some reason we are not shown on Grab Maps !)

- if you arrive by motorbike just search for “ Sudamala Ubud Permaculture ” and you will find us
  - link: "Sudamala Ubud Permaculture" → https://maps.app.goo.gl/PuRAqkHBQwfDNKh16
- if you arrive by car search for “ Pura Dalem Gentong “. Take the path to the left of the temple and then follow the signs as we are 300 metres up a small track !
